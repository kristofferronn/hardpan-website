What kind of game it is

Build

Found a village on an empty shore and grow it into a palace town. You choose where every house, well, field and workshop goes, and since nothing teleports, every layout is a decision about who walks how far.

Experience

Everything is one to one. Every person has a home, a trade and a family; every jug of water is fired at your kiln and carried on someone's back; every building and field stands at true scale. You watch a Bronze Age town work, person by person.

Survive

Then the world starts to come apart. Droughts first, then raiders and broken trade routes. Survive the Bronze Age Collapse, one disaster after another, and take your palace economy apart in time to save the people inside it.

Where it stands

Collapse is in early development at Hardpan Games, a one-person studio. I design it and make the art by hand, without generative AI; AI tools help with much of the code.

The grey box now plays a full founding run. Four settlers, a pile of cargo and a spring: you choose where to live, build the first houses and fields, and grow a village through the seasons, with barley and grass peas, goats on the hill, fish from the shore and oxen at the plough. Every load is carried by hand, families arrive and children grow up. Then the rains fail, and the question becomes who you can keep alive.

Next: village life (feasts, a communal tomb, tools that wear out) and the first playtests with outside players in November, while the final art is made alongside.

A Kickstarter is planned for spring 2027, once a playable piece of the game looks the way it should. Sign up to hear when it goes live.
